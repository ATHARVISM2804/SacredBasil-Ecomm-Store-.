# Sacred Basil — Abhyanga Oil Reel (55s) · Shot list & AI prompts

Format: vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 55 seconds. Music + short text overlays, no voiceover.
Every shot is AI-generated. The `public/video/oil*` clips are reference only.
Product: **30 ml** Aromatic Body Oil. Finished oil colour: **deep amber** (as in `oil6.jpeg`).

## How to make each AI shot (free)

1. **Image** — open Gemini (gemini.google.com), paste the **Style anchor** + the shot's **Image prompt**. Ask for "vertical 9:16". Generate 3–4, keep the best.
2. **Video** — upload that image to **Kling** (klingai.com) or **Hailuo** (hailuoai.video) → *Image to Video*, 5s, 9:16. Paste the shot's **Motion prompt** and the **Negative prompt**. Generate 2–3, keep the best.
3. **Save** it as `reel/shots/s01.mp4`, `s02.mp4`… (the shot number). 5s is fine — I trim to length.

Rules that keep it premium:
- Same Style anchor on **every** image, word for word. This is what makes 12 AI clips look like one film.
- Slow camera only. If a clip morphs, warps or flickers, regenerate — don't keep it.
- Bottle shots (S13, S15, S16) are **image-to-image**: upload `reel/reference/bottle-30ml-single.jpg` (or `-row.jpg`) to Gemini together with the prompt, so it copies the real bottle and label. Reject any image where the label text is wrong or misspelled — zoom in and check "Aromatic Body Oil", "अभ्यंग तेल" and "30 ml".
- For bottle shots, use the gentlest motion and keep the bottle still. Movement is where AI melts labels.

### Style anchor (paste before every image prompt)

> Luxury Ayurvedic skincare campaign photography. Warm soft morning window light from the left, gentle deep shadows. Palette of golden amber, aged brass, cream linen, deep wine and sage green. Dark textured stone surface, handwoven cream linen cloth, antique brass vessels. Shallow depth of field, 85mm macro lens, subtle film grain, calm and meditative mood. Vertical 9:16. No text, no logos, no people's faces.

### Negative prompt (paste in Kling / Hailuo)

> text, letters, watermark, logo, label, distorted hands, extra fingers, morphing, warping, flicker, fast camera movement, zoom burst, plastic look, cartoon, oversaturated, low quality

## AI shots — prompts

### S01 · Hook — golden oil drop (0.0–4.0)
**Image:** Extreme macro of a single thick drop of golden amber oil suspended just above a still pool of golden oil in a small antique brass bowl, backlit by warm window light so the oil glows, tiny basil leaf resting at the bowl's edge, dark background.
**Motion:** The drop falls in extreme slow motion and touches the oil surface, a soft ripple spreads outward. Camera completely still. Light shimmers through the oil.

### S02 · Sesame seeds (4.0–6.5)
**Image:** Close-up of pale golden sesame seeds piled in a small brass bowl on cream linen, a scattering of seeds on the dark stone, warm side light catching each seed.
**Motion:** A gentle stream of sesame seeds falls from above into the bowl in slow motion, seeds bouncing softly. Very slow push-in.

### S03 · Sesame oil pour (6.5–9.5)
**Image:** A thin glossy stream of golden sesame oil pouring from a small brass lota into a wide shallow brass urli, warm backlight making the oil stream glow, sesame seeds scattered nearby.
**Motion:** Slow-motion steady oil stream pouring, gentle swirl forming in the urli. Slow camera tilt down following the stream.

### S04 · Yashtimadhu (9.5–12.5)

**Motion:** Very slow push-in and slight rotation. Fine dust particles drift in the light beam.
