# Sacred Basil product photography: ChatGPT enhancement prompts

## How to use

- **One image per chat.** Attach the original photo (and the extra reference where noted), paste the **Style block**, then the image's own prompt.
- Save results to `docs/product-photos/enhanced/` with the **same filename** (e.g. `05-tranquil-box-open-4-products.png`).
- **Formats:**
  - **Card** = 4:5 portrait (1600 × 2000), used for product cards and product pages.
  - **Wide** = 3:2 landscape (2400 × 1600), used for story sections and banners.
- If a label comes out misspelt or a product changes, reply with: `Fix only this: [problem]. Keep everything else identical.`

## Style block (paste with every prompt)

```
Recreate this exact product as a premium still-life photograph for the Sacred Basil website.

PRODUCT FIDELITY (most important): keep the product, packaging, label design, label text, logo, colours, shape, size and the exact number of items identical to the attached photo. Do not add, remove, duplicate or redesign any product. Copy every label word exactly. If text is unreadable, keep it soft rather than inventing words.

SET: seamless warm ivory-to-sand backdrop (#EFE6DA to #D9C8B0), one honed travertine plinth or stone surface, warm golden-hour key light from the upper left with long soft shadows and a gentle rim light on glass and gold. Restrained props only: at most one small sprig of dried gypsophila and a few marigold or rose petals. Generous negative space.

CAMERA: medium-format, 85 mm, f/4, shallow depth of field, creamy highlights, rich warm shadows, subtle film grain. Palette: ivory, sand, chocolate brown, brushed gold.

AVOID: people (unless stated), hands (unless stated), clutter, busy patterns, offices, newspapers, plastic bags, glossy CGI, oversaturated orange, any extra text or watermark.
```
