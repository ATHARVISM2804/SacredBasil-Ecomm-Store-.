# Studio photographs v6 (November 2026)

Every product photograph on the site after the November revamp. All are made in Gemini as **edits of a real reference**, never from text alone, so the packaging matches the brochure:

- `ref/`: crops of the brochure pages (`docs/inputs/pdf-pages`), the reference for the four gift sets.
- `refjpg/`: the same as JPEGs, plus `s-*` (the real studio-range photos from `docs/regen2/send`) and `c-*` / `gen-*` (approved results reused as references for the group shot).
- `incoming/`: Gemini's downloads as they came. Rejected tries are kept (for example `luxe-dark-v1`, misspelled lid; `group-v1/v2`, "Diwali Down").
- `clean/`: approved images with Gemini's sparkle mark removed (smooth paper fill, nothing else touched).
- `hero/`: hero frames (`d-*` 8:3, `m-*` 4:5). The paper was extended in code, so the products are untouched.

Studio look for every shot: seamless warm ivory paper (#EEE8E0), or deep chocolate for the Luxe banner; one soft key light from the upper left; no flowers, petals, fabric or props.

Every image was checked against the brochure for box colour, foil, lid text, tray layout and label spelling before it went into `public/images/v6`. Known small flaw: on a few labels, the tiny "SACRED BASIL" line under the SB monogram is not letter-perfect. It is unreadable at site sizes.
